Updated: 2026-11-17

IIDeletion and your rights

You can burn a question in the diary: its text and handwriting are wiped from the phone and the server at once. To delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete account: the data on the server, the link to your Apple ID and purchases, and the diary and settings on the phone are wiped, and analytics events lose their link to you. Data leaves backups within 30 days. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

To find out what data we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, write to flyyyxxxy@gmail.com. In the EU you have rights under the GDPR: access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and complaint to a supervisory authority.
