IIIYour account

On first launch an anonymous account is created — there is nothing to fill in. You can sign in with Apple later so your diary moves with you to a new phone. You can delete the account in Settings: deletion wipes everything on the server and on the phone and cannot be undone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

IVFree and with a subscription

Always free: the card of the day, one three-card spread as a gift, the diary with your daily cards and the gift spread, the base deck.

A subscription adds spreads for your own question (all four layouts), a reading for your question, the full diary, wax seals, a one-card follow-up, extra decks and the 79th card. A subscriber can make up to 10 spreads a day.

  • Week — $3.99, month — $9.99, year — $59.99. Prices are in US dollars; in other countries the App Store shows the price in local currency.
  • The first 7 days are free on the yearly plan only. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial week ends, the yearly price is charged.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

VReadings are written by a model

A reading for your question is written by Anthropic’s Claude language model. It can be wrong, repeat itself or misread the question. If a reading feels off, there is a “Something’s wrong” link at the bottom of the sheet — that is how we learn about it.

The deck doesn’t take some questions: if a question shows signs of acute distress, there will be no spread — the app shows helplines for your App Store country instead, or an international helpline directory if your country isn’t on the list. That is not a diagnosis or a judgement; it is care.
