ICommon questions

What the subscription gives and what it costs

The card of the day is always free, and your first three-card spread is a gift. A subscription adds spreads for your own question, a reading for your question, the full diary, wax seals, a one-card follow-up, new decks and the 79th card. Week — $3.99, month — $9.99, year — $59.99; the yearly plan starts with 7 days free. In other countries the App Store shows the price in local currency.

How to cancel the subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tarocchi → Cancel Subscription. Or in the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it will renew. The free week of the yearly plan is cancelled the same way — then nothing is charged.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

How to get a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but spreads are still locked

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases” — the same button is on the plans sheet. The phone must be signed in to the same Apple ID that bought the subscription.

How to move the diary to a new phone

On the old phone: Settings → Account → Sign in with Apple. After that the diary copy on the server is tied to your Apple ID and will be found on a new phone.

On the new phone: on the first screen tap “Sign in with Apple” under “Has the deck been with you on another phone?” — or sign in later in Settings. The envelopes arrive newest first; the rest catch up.

Without Sign in with Apple the diary is tied to this phone and can’t be moved.

How to delete your account

Settings → Account → Delete account; the app asks once more. Everything is wiped: the diary copy, questions and readings on the server, the link to your Apple ID and purchases, and the diary and settings on this phone. The table becomes clean, as on the first day. Deletion can’t be undone.

Deleting does not cancel a subscription — if you have one, cancel it in the App Store.

Do the cards predict the future?

No. Tarocchi is for entertainment and reflection. The cards don’t predict the future and don’t replace a doctor, therapist, lawyer or financial adviser. A reading is a way to look at your question from another side; the decisions stay yours.

A reading came out strange

At the bottom of the reading there is a “Something’s wrong” link: pick a reason — we receive the reason and the spread’s number and look into what went wrong.

IIIf things are hard right now

If you feel very bad or have thoughts of hurting yourself, cards won’t help here — people will. Call your local emergency number (112 in Europe and Russia, 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK) or a crisis line in your country; in the US you can call or text 988. Free, anonymous helplines by country are listed at findahelpline.com.

If the app sees signs of acute distress in a question, it won’t make a spread and will show helplines for your App Store country instead, or an international helpline directory if your country isn’t on the list.
